Held-out Library

Winter opening hours

Holiday closures: the library is shut from the 24th to the 27th and on New Year's Day.

The reading room opens an hour earlier from the first Monday of the month, and the returns desk moves to the ground floor beside the east door.

Members who reserved a desk for the autumn keep it until the end of January. New reservations open on the fifteenth and are handed out in the order they arrive.

The map collection stays on the second floor. Large sheets are brought down on request, usually within twenty minutes, and may be photographed without flash.

Lockers by the entrance take a one pound coin, which is returned. Coats and umbrellas are not allowed in the reading room after the incident with the atlas.

The courtyard cafe closes at four. Hot drinks with lids may be taken to the benches in the lobby but no further, and the staff do check.

Volunteers are needed on Saturday mornings to shelve returns. Training takes one hour and comes with a library tote bag and a quiet sense of purpose.
