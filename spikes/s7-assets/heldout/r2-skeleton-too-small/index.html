The allotment society meets on the second Thursday of each month in the pavilion. Tea is provided; biscuits are a matter for the members.

Plots are let by the year from the first of March. Half plots are offered first to people on the waiting list who have asked for one.

Water is turned on at the standpipes in April and off again at the first frost. Hoses may be used before nine and after six only.

Bonfires are allowed on the first Saturday of the month between October and March, when the wind is not towards the houses.

The shared shed holds two wheelbarrows, a strimmer and the scales for the autumn show. The key hangs inside the pavilion door.

Manure is delivered in February. Order it through the secretary by the end of January and pay when it arrives.
