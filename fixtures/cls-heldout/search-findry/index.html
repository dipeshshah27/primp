People also ask

How long does a sourdough starter take to be ready?

Usually between seven and fourteen days, depending on the flour and the temperature of the room.

Can I use tap water for my starter?

Yes, unless it is heavily chlorinated, in which case leave it to stand for an hour first.

Why does my starter smell like nail varnish?

It is hungry. Feed it more often or with a higher ratio of fresh flour.
