Questions

Is there a free trial?

Thirty days on any plan, with no card needed until you decide to stay.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades at the end of the billing period.

Do you charge per invoice?

No. Invoices, quotes and bills are unlimited on every plan.

Accountant seats are free on Team and Group and do not count as users.
