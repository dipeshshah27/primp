Corpus Gazette

Route 9: station to the old tram shed

Diversion this weekend

Route 9 does not serve the market square on Saturday. Use the stop outside the library instead.

Buses leave the station every twelve minutes on weekdays and every fifteen at the weekend. The journey to the tram shed takes about a quarter of an hour.

The route passes the hospital, the library and the market square. All stops have a shelter and a printed timetable; the four busiest also show live times.

The first bus leaves the station at ten to six and the last at half past eleven. On Friday and Saturday nights the night bus takes over on the same stops.

Low-floor buses run on this route. The space by the middle doors takes two wheelchairs or prams, and drivers will lower the ramp on request.

A single ticket costs the same from any stop. Day tickets bought on this route are valid on the whole network and on the harbour ferry.
