Your account

Sign in to see the tickets you have bought, the journeys you have saved and the stop you chose as your home stop. Your season ticket shows as a code that the driver can scan.

If you change your phone, sign in on the new one and the tickets move with you. A ticket can be shown on one phone at a time.

You can add a second traveller to a family ticket from this page. They will get their own code by email and do not need an account.

To close your account, write to the travel shop. Unused tickets are refunded to the card that paid for them, less the cost of any journeys already made.

We keep journey history for ninety days so that you can claim a refund for a delayed bus. After that it is deleted and cannot be recovered.
