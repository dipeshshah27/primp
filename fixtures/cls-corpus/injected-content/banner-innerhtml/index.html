Harbour ferry fares for the winter season

Single fares stay at the summer price until the new year. A return bought before nine in the morning costs the same as two singles; after nine it is a fifth cheaper.

Books of ten tickets are sold at the office on the north pier and can be shared between people travelling together. They do not expire.

Children under five travel free. Older children pay half fare, and a family ticket covers two adults and up to three children for the whole day.

Bicycles travel free outside the two peak hours. Motorcycles are carried on the larger boat only and need a vehicle ticket, which must be bought in advance.

Refunds for cancelled crossings are paid at the ticket office or, for tickets bought by card on board, returned to the card within five working days.
