Sea conditions today

The forecast panel below is filled in by a script once the observations have been fetched. Until then a short grey placeholder stands in for it.

Small craft should check the forecast before leaving the breakwater. The harbour office hoists a red flag when the wind at the pier head is above force six.

The ferry sails in anything up to force seven from the west. Easterly winds close the island landing sooner, because the swell runs straight onto the steps.

Tide times on this site are corrected for the inner basin, where high water is about ten minutes later than at the pier head.

Fog signals sound from the lighthouse whenever visibility drops below two miles. The signal is one long blast every thirty seconds.
