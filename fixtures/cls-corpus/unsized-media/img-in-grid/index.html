Corpus Gazette

  • Tide tables

    High and low water for the next seven days, corrected for the inner basin.

  • East wall moorings

    Twelve berths reopen after the dredging, with new ladders and shore power.

  • Notices to mariners

    Three new notices this week, including the yellow visitor buoys.

  • Lifeboat open day

    The station opens its doors on the last Saturday of the month.

  • Fuel berth hours

    Closed on Sunday afternoons until the spring; diesel on request.

  • Ticket office

    Opens half an hour before the first departure, every day of the week.

The harbour authority publishes these pages every Friday. Corrections can be sent by post or left at the ticket office on the north pier.
