Harbour ferry timetable changes from Monday

The morning crossing moves fifteen minutes earlier so that it meets the first train. Passengers who hold a season ticket do not need to do anything; the new times apply automatically.

The last evening sailing stays where it is. On Fridays an extra boat leaves at half past eleven, which is the service people asked for most often in the spring survey.

Bicycles travel free on every crossing outside the two peak hours. During the peak, the lower deck takes twelve bicycles and the crew will ask the rest to wait for the next boat.

The ticket office on the north pier opens half an hour before the first departure. Card payments work on board, and paper tickets bought before the change remain valid until the end of the year.

Weather cancellations are announced on the pier boards and by text message. A cancelled crossing is replaced by a bus that follows the coast road and takes about forty minutes longer.

The operator reviews the timetable again in March. Comments can be left at the ticket office or sent by post to the harbour authority, which publishes a summary of what it receives.

Dogs are welcome on the upper deck. The café on the larger boat is open on every crossing that lasts longer than twenty minutes and takes both cash and cards.
