Corpus Gazette

Today's departures

Times are shown for the north pier. Add ten minutes for the south quay and twenty-five for the island landing, where the boat waits for the returning passengers.

Boarding closes two minutes before departure. The gangway is level with the pier at half tide and steep at low water, when the crew will help with prams and bicycles.

A cancelled crossing is replaced by a bus that follows the coast road. The bus leaves from the stop outside the ticket office at the time the boat would have sailed.

Season tickets are valid on both boats. Single tickets bought on board cost slightly more than those bought at the office or from the machine on the pier.
