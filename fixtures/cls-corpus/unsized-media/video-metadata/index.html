Corpus Gazette

Watch: the new lock gates being lowered in

The two gates were built upriver and floated down on a barge during the night, when the canal is closed to traffic. Each one weighs about as much as six buses.

A crane on the east bank lifts a gate, turns it through a quarter circle and lowers it onto pins that were set into the lock wall last month.

Divers check the lower hinge before the crane lets go. The whole lift takes under an hour, most of which is spent waiting for the gate to stop swinging.

The lock reopens at the weekend. Boats that were held upstream will be let through in the order they arrived, starting with the working barges.

The old gates, which had been in the water for forty years, go to the canal museum, where one of them will stand in the yard.
