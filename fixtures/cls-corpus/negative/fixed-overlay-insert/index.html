Overlays that sit on top of the page

A consent bar is inserted as the first child of the body 700 ms after load, exactly as on the cookie-bar page of this corpus, but here it is fixed to the bottom of the viewport.

A toast is appended inside the main column at the same moment. It is absolutely positioned, so it covers part of the heading without pushing it.

Both are new elements. Nothing that was already on screen changes position, so no layout shift is reported.

This is the usual fix for the injected-content pages: take the late element out of the flow, or reserve its space.
