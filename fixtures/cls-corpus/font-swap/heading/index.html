Corpus Gazette

Night buses return this winter

The ring road service starts again on the first Friday of December and runs every half hour between midnight and five in the morning, seven nights a week.

Buses stop at every shelter on the ring and wait for up to three minutes at the station, so that passengers from the last train can make the connection.

Day tickets bought after six in the evening stay valid until the first morning bus. Season tickets and concession passes are accepted without a supplement.

The council paid for the service after a petition signed by hospital staff, who finish their late shift after the last ordinary bus has gone.

Drivers have asked for two of the darker stops to be lit before the service begins. The lighting contractor expects to finish the work in the last week of November.

Passenger numbers will be counted for three months. If the buses carry fewer than forty people a night the timetable drops to one bus an hour.
