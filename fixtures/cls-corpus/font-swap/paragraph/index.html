Corpus Gazette

The market moves indoors for the winter

From the first Saturday of November the weekly market sets up in the old tram shed behind the station. Forty stalls fit under the roof, the doors open at eight, and the bakers have promised to bring twice as much bread as they did last year.

The shed has been empty since the trams stopped running. The council cleared it in the summer, laid a level floor and put in lights that can be dimmed for the evening events.

Stallholders pay the same pitch fee as they do in the square. Those who sell hot food are grouped by the east doors, where the extractor fans are.

There is no parking at the shed. The nearest car park is behind the library, five minutes away on foot, and the number nine bus stops outside.

The market goes back to the square in the last week of March, weather permitting. The shed will then be used for the spring plant sale.
