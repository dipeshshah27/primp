Planning a journey

Every route on the network is drawn on the map at the main stops, with the first and last bus of the day printed underneath the stop name.

Timetables change twice a year, at the end of March and at the end of October. The printed booklets are free from the travel shop at the station.

A day ticket covers all buses and the harbour ferry. Children under eleven travel free with an adult, and dogs travel free at any time.

Low-floor buses run on every route. Drivers will lower the ramp on request and the space by the middle doors takes two wheelchairs or prams.

Things left on a bus are taken to the depot overnight and to the travel shop the next morning, where they are kept for a month.
