The lighthouse keeper retires

After thirty-one winters on the rock, the last resident keeper on this coast handed over the keys on Friday. The light has been automatic for years; she stayed to look after the building.

You learn the weather by the sound of the door.

The trust that owns the lighthouse will open it to visitors from the spring. The lamp room, the kitchen and the logbooks stay as they are.

Boats to the rock leave from the fishing quay on calm days only. The crossing takes forty minutes and the landing is by ladder.

The keeper is moving to a house on the mainland from which, she says, she can still see the light if she stands on a chair.

Her logbooks, which record the wind and the sea state four times a day, are being copied by volunteers at the town library.
