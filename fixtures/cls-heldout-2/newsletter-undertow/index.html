What the dredger found

Sunday 8 March 2026 · 9 minute read
Every harbour is an argument with silt, and the silt has more time than we do. All a dredger can win is another ten years of tides.

The spoil from a deepened channel is a core sample of a town: clinker, clay pipes, a century of dropped spanners. This fortnight I spent a morning on a dredger with a woman who can date a mooring chain by its rust.
