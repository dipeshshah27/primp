Change the owner of a workspace

Written by Camille Osei · Updated 4 March 2026 · 3 min read

Every workspace has exactly one owner. The owner pays for the plan, can delete the workspace and is the only person who can hand it to somebody else.

Before you start. The new owner must already be an admin of the workspace and must have confirmed their email address.

Transfer ownership

  1. Open Settings and choose Workspace.
  2. Under Ownership, select Transfer.
  3. Pick the admin who should become the owner and type the workspace name to confirm.
  4. The new owner receives an email and has seven days to accept.

What changes, and what does not

Billing moves to the new owner at the start of the next invoice period. Open tickets, automations and API keys are not affected. You become an admin and can be removed like any other member.

Press G then S anywhere in Brightdesk to jump to Settings.
