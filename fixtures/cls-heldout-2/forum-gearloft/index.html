Going from a 36 to a 42 the upper jockey wheel will be sitting far too close to the big sprockets. Wind the B-tension screw in until there is a 5–6 mm gap on the 42:

gap on largest sprocket: 5-6 mm
b-tension: clockwise = more gap
check: shift 42 -> 37 -> 42 under load

Then re-index, because moving the B-screw shifts everything slightly.
