The lighthouse keeper's logbook

12 March 2026 · 47 min · — plays
08:27 / 47:02

Show notes

For thirty-one years the keeper at Skerry Head wrote down the weather four times a day, and in the margins, everything else: wrecks, visitors, the price of paraffin and a long-running argument with the Board about a cat.

We read the logbooks with the archivist who rescued them from a skip, and follow one entry from 1953 to the family it was written about.
