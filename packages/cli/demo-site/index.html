Product

Everything on-call needs. Nothing it doesn't.

One place for checks, alerts and the status page your customers read, built for the engineer who gets paged at 3 a.m.

  • Checks from 32 regions

    HTTP, TCP, DNS and browser checks every 30 seconds, confirmed from three regions before anyone is woken up.

  • Alerts that route themselves

    Schedules, escalations and overrides. Pages go to whoever is on call, by phone, SMS, Slack or push.

  • Traces with every alert

    Each failed check carries its request, response and the slowest span, so the first minute goes to fixing, not finding.

  • Status pages

    A public page on your own domain that updates itself when an incident opens, and subscribers who hear about it first.

  • Cron and heartbeats

    One curl at the end of a job. Hear about the backup that never ran, not just the endpoint that fell over.

  • Certificates and domains

    Expiry warnings 30, 14 and 3 days out for every TLS certificate and domain you own, found for you.
